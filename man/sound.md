## DESCRIPTION

List the sound cards and choose which one plays sound. Sound plays on
the card added last, so a USB headset or Bluetooth headphones take over
when they appear and hand back when they go.

## COMMANDS

- `sound`, `sound list`: the sound cards, with a `*` on the one playing
  sound.
- `use N`: always play on card N.
- `use bluetooth`: play on the Bluetooth headphones.
- `use auto`: play on the card added last again.
- `test`: play three notes, to check you can hear them.
