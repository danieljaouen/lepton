## DESCRIPTION

Show or change the sound volume: one volume for everything, Lepton's
own sounds and Firefox's alike. The volume and mute are remembered
across restarts on an installed system. The volume keys of a keyboard
work on the desktop too.

## OPTIONS

- (none): print the volume, whether it is muted, and the sound card.
- `N`: set the volume to N percent (0 to 100).
- `+N`, `-N`: make it N louder or quieter.
- `up`, `down`: 5 louder or quieter.
- `mute`, `unmute`, `toggle`: mute, unmute, or switch between them.

## EXAMPLES

    volume 75
    volume +10
    volume mute
