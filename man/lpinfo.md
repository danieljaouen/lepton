## DESCRIPTION

List the IPP printers found on the network right now (by mDNS, as
AirPrint finds them), with their addresses, names and models. They can
be printed to as they are; `lpadmin` gives one a name of your choosing.

## OPTIONS

- `-v`: list the printers (the only listing).

printers(1), lpadmin(1), lpstat(1)
