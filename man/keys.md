## DESCRIPTION

Show the key bindings Lepton loaded from your dotfiles: the editor's
(Spacemacs bindings from `~/.spacemacs` and friends), tmux's (from
`~/.tmux.conf`, with its prefix key) and git's aliases (from
`~/.gitconfig`). For the shell, it points at `alias` and `bindkey`. Bindings Lepton does not
support are marked.

## OPTIONS

- `editor` (or `emacs`, `spacemacs`), `tmux`, `shell`, `git`: show only
  that set.
